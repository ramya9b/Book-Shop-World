Our Story

A little bakery built on butter, sugar & love

Sweet Crumbs began as a home kitchen and grew into the neighbourhood's favourite bake shop. We mix every batter by hand, bake in small batches, and finish each cake with care — just like we would for our own family celebrations.

From custom birthday cakes to your daily loaf of bread, everything is made fresh, never frozen.
